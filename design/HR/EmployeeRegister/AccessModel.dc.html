Gate by permission, never by role name

Access model

Every column and link on this page is gated on the same permission the destination route already declares in core/security/route-permissions.ts. A link the viewer cannot follow is not rendered — it never renders disabled, and it never leads to a 403.

Two gates, not one

The route guard keeps the page shut. The cell and link gates decide what a viewer who got in may see and reach. Both run — the page gate alone would let a Shop Manager read PII.

Hide links, mask cells

An action the viewer cannot take is absent. A column they cannot read stays, showing “Restricted” — the grid keeps its shape, and a gate never reads as missing data.

The UI is not the policy

Gates here are a courtesy, not enforcement — the backend refuses regardless. Where policy is conditional, read the server’s capability flags rather than inferring from a role name.

The personas are illustrations of permission sets, not gates in their own right. A tenant can mint a role holding any subset of these codes, and the page must render correctly for all of them — which is why every row above names a permission, and why none of them names a role.
