Page states

state() === 'loading' aria-busy, skeleton rows keep the grid from jumping
state() === 'empty' filters returned nothing — offer the way back
No employees match “byrd, terminated”
Try a different term, or clear the status filter.
state() === 'error' set before errorKey(); message comes from the key, never the raw response
We couldn’t load the employee list
PEOPLE.EMPLOYEE_REGISTER.ERROR.LOAD — the service is unreachable. Your filters are kept.
state() === 'forbidden' a 403 the route guard let through — never a blank page
You don’t have access to the employee register
This page needs people:employee:view. The People directory is open to you and carries name, username, email and phone.

A permission the viewer lacks removes the column’s data and the action, never the column — the grid keeps its shape so a shared screenshot still lines up. Masked cells read “Restricted”, not an empty dash, so nobody mistakes a gate for missing data.
